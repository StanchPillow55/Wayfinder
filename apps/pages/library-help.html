Library card help

Answers about getting and using a city library card.

Questions

When is the library open?

Branches are open Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.

How do I get a card?

Bring a photo ID and a current address to any branch desk.

How do I renew a book?

Renew online or at the desk unless another person has requested the book.

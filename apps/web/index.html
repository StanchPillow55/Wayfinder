    Braille

    Grade 1 Braille of the live words, drawn as raised pins. Drag the display, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to turn it. This software view is not the motor, and it is not a physical Braille device.

    Command

    While Wayfinder is speaking, tap an option, start talking, or press Stop, Back, or a new command. The sentence stops immediately and that choice runs. The session stays. “Just do it for me” takes the safe steps toward your goal and still waits for your own yes before anything is submitted. Try: find the hours, request the north room, add the talking kitchen scale to my cart, actually take me to the cart.

    Press 1 through 5 for an option when you are not typing and no confirmation is open. Escape declines a waiting confirmation.

    Output and channels

    Channels, not settings about a person. Speech, the page, Braille, and the motor cue can all be on together. Tactile is an extra sensation alongside the page. The choices stay on screen. There is nothing to set up.

              Hands-free modes

              The first click, tap, or key anywhere turns the microphone, spoken audio, and camera on together. They stay on. Arm is not a switch. Mute and Turn camera off are separate.

              Use by voice

              Voice is off until the first click, tap, or key. Then say a command such as “what is here”.

              Say: what is here, next, previous, confirm, back, stop, wait, what did you just say, explain that, less detail, read everything, where was I, forget that constraint, just do it for me, find the hours, request the north room, open shop, open jobs, open faq, open example dot org, switch to braille, mute, or a number from 1 to 5. Confirm on an option that needs confirmation takes two spoken confirms: the first chooses it, the second says yes. Unknown speech is never executed. Mute microphone and Mute spoken audio are separate from arming. This uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome that audio goes to the browser vendor's service, so it is not offline. If your browser has none, the typed command box still works.

              Use by looking

              Camera is off.

              No option is highlighted by the camera selector.

              Motion 0.0. Waiting.

              Laptop-camera demo. This is not gaze tracking, it is not a blink detector, and it is not the P4X-EYE. Put your face in the box. A clearly large nod, lean, or wave moves to the next option. A longer large move chooses the highlighted option. Small expression changes, blinks, and brief motion do nothing. Becoming still after an expression does not choose anything, so an empty chair or a blank stare cannot choose either. An option that needs confirmation needs that longer move a second time. Two quick moves decline. It can fail in dim or changing light, with a moving background, or with a camera that shifts. The video stays in this tab. No image is stored or sent, and none goes into the page model. If the browser's Face Detector is missing, a fixed box is used and nobody checks that a face is in it. A second press does not turn the camera off. Turn camera off is the only stop.

              Demo map, not a clinical device. Buttons and the analog input on the DevKit are unverified pin assumptions, and the controls above are on-screen stand-ins that send the same actions. The cue shows the planned duty, duration, and word. It does not prove the motor rotated, it does not prove a blind user can complete a task, and this is not a physical Braille device.

              Open a page

              Opening the library help page.

              Local pages are served to a headless Chromium that Playwright drives. A web address you type is opened the same way and read by its structure. Sites that are down, need a sign-in, or show a CAPTCHA are reported honestly and left unchanged. Wayfinder does not enter passwords or card numbers, and the local pages are always available. Harbor Line is the labeled regression fixture and offline fallback, not the live browser.

                                  Tactile

                                  The bar is the software tactile view of a motor run: duty, duration, and direction for the current pose. The 130 mini DC brushless motor spins, it does not hold an angle. A physical run is claimed only when a DevKit answers on USB, and none has. Target under 250 ms has not been measured on hardware.

                                  Quiet glance

                                    A text-only, camera-free glance of the same state, a few short lines for a small surface. It is a software projection. No glasses hardware or SDK is attached.

                                    Hardware topology

                                      The Mac holds canonical state. Each board has its own USB link. Nothing here uses a UART cable between an ESP32-S3 and an ESP32-C3.
